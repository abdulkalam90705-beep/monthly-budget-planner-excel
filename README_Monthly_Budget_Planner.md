# Monthly Budget Planner – Excel Project

## Overview
A Microsoft Excel budget workbook covering five expense categories across January–April 2026. The workbook includes monthly amounts, category totals, percentage shares, summary statistics, and a pie chart.

## Dataset
- Period: January–April 2026
- Categories: Rent, Car Payment, Insurance, Phone, Candy
- Total recorded amount: ₹10,435
- Highest month: April (₹2,720)
- Largest category: Rent (₹4,800; 46.00%)

## Features shown in the workbook
- Monthly expense tracking
- Category-wise totals and percentage shares
- Average, minimum, maximum and count summary
- Pie chart
- Separate chart worksheet and reusable template layout

## Tools and skills
- Microsoft Excel
- Worksheet formatting and data organization
- Basic formulas and summary statistics
- Percentage calculations
- Chart creation and worksheet management

## Key findings
- Total recorded spending for the four months is ₹10,435.
- April has the highest monthly total: ₹2,720.
- Rent is the largest category: ₹4,800 (46.00%).
- Insurance is the second-largest category: ₹2,800 (26.83%).
